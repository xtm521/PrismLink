Support & FAQ / 技术支持

iPhone (iOS 18+) & Mac (macOS 15+ Apple Silicon) Pure Apple Native Companion

Quick Start / 快速上手

  1. Download and install the notarized PrismLink Host for macOS into /Applications.
  2. Open PrismLink Host → Overview and grant Screen & System Audio Recording and Accessibility in macOS System Settings.
  3. Sign in with Apple on iPhone and Mac, then scan the signed QR code or verify the 6-digit SAS security code to pin your hardware key in Keychain.

Troubleshooting / 常见问题

  • Permissions still show orange after enabling in System Settings? Remove any stale PrismLink Host entry with - in System Settings → Privacy & Security, add /Applications/PrismLink Host.app again with +, then click Restart to Refresh Permissions inside PrismLink Host.
  • Emergency Disconnect (Kill Switch): Press ⌘ + ⌥ + ⇧ + Esc on your Mac at any time to immediately disconnect the remote session and restore physical display & input state.
  • Manage or Cancel Subscription: Open iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions at any time.
  • Erase Account & CloudKit Data: Open PrismLink on iPhone → Settings → Delete Account & Erase iCloud Data.
