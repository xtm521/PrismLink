Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective Date / 生效日期：2026-11-27

1. Privacy Policy (English)

PrismLink operates zero third-party account servers, media relay servers, ad SDKs, analytics trackers, or AI proxy backends. Your display video, system audio, input events, clipboard, terminal sessions, local development tool commands, and file transfers travel directly peer-to-peer between your iPhone and your own Mac under Post-Quantum X-Wing (ML-KEM-768 + X25519) + ChaCha20-Poly1305 End-to-End Encryption.

  • Local Mac Tool Integration (No Cloud AI Proxy): PrismLink does not bundle, host, or proxy any third-party cloud AI API. When you interact with local command-line or desktop coding tools installed on your own Mac, prompts and outputs remain strictly between your iPhone and your Mac.
  • Sign in with Apple & iCloud Private Database (CloudKit): Used solely to discover Macs signed in to your own Apple Account, exchange short-lived signed P2P signaling candidates, and sync Apple-signed StoreKit 2 Pro entitlements across your own devices. All records reside exclusively inside your personal Apple iCloud Private Database; the developer has zero access.
  • Local Keychain Hardware Pinning: Paired device public keys and 6-digit SAS verification fingerprints are stored locally in Apple Keychain (kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly) and never leave your device.
  • On-Device Permissions:
    • Camera (iOS): Used only to scan your Mac's signed pairing QR code.
    • Face ID / Touch ID (iOS): Used locally to authorize remote connections and high-risk command approvals; biometric data never leaves the device.
    • Microphone & Speech Recognition (iOS): Used strictly for on-device Apple Speech Recognition dictation (requiresOnDeviceRecognition) when dictating text prompts; audio is never uploaded to PrismLink servers.
    • Local Network (iOS & macOS): Used for Bonjour discovery and native QUIC peer-to-peer streaming on your local network.
    • Screen Recording & Accessibility (macOS): Used exclusively on your Mac to stream your chosen display to your paired iPhone and inject remote keyboard/mouse input you initiate.
  • Account & Data Deletion: Open Settings → Delete Account & Erase iCloud Data inside PrismLink at any time to permanently erase all CloudKit Private Database records and revoke all local Keychain pins.
  • Subscriptions & Payments: All purchases are processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit 2. PrismLink collects no payment information. Apple's Standard EULA applies: Apple Standard EULA.

二、隐私政策（简体中文）

PrismLink 不运营任何第三方账号服务器、视频中转服务器、广告统计 SDK 或云端 AI 代理后台。 您的屏幕画面、系统音频、键鼠输入、剪贴板、终端会话、本地开发工具指令与文件传输均在您的 iPhone 与自有 Mac 之间点对点（P2P）直接传输，并全程受 后量子 X-Wing（ML-KEM-768 + X25519）与 ChaCha20-Poly1305 端到端加密保护。

  • 仅连接用户本机工具（零云端 AI 中转）：PrismLink 自身不调用也不转发任何第三方云端 AI 接口，仅在您的 iPhone 与您自有 Mac 上已安装的本地工具间安全传递指令与结果。
  • Apple ID 登录与 iCloud 私有库（CloudKit Private Database）：仅用于发现您同一 Apple 账户下的 Mac 设备、交换短期签名网络候选地址以及跨设备同步 StoreKit 2 订阅凭证。所有数据仅保存在您个人的 Apple iCloud 私有空间中，开发者无法读取。
  • 本机权限用途：相机仅用于扫码配对；面容 ID 仅用于本机解锁与高危命令确认；麦克风与语音识别默认使用 Apple 设备端离线识别（语音不离开 iPhone）；本地网络用于局域网 QUIC 直连；macOS 录屏与辅助功能仅用于画面串流与响应您的远程操作。
  • 一键清除账户与数据：您可随时在 iPhone 端「设置 → 删除账户并清空 iCloud 数据」彻底删除全部 CloudKit 记录与本地钥匙串设备固定。
